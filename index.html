⏰ Ingliz tili Zamonlari

1. Present Simple
Odatlar va doimiy takrorlanadigan ishlar uchun.
(+): V / Vs
(-): don't / doesn't + V
(?): Do / Does + S + V
2. Present Continuous
Hozirgi vaqtda sodir bo'layotgan jarayonlar.
(+): am/is/are + V-ing
(-): am/is/are + not + V-ing
(?): Am/Is/Are + S + V-ing
3. Past Simple
O'tgan zamondagi tugallangan ish-harakatlar.
(+): V2 / V-ed
(-): didn't + V
(?): Did + S + V
4. Future Simple
Kelajakda bajariladigan ish-harakatlar.
(+): will + V
(-): won't + V
(?): Will + S + V
